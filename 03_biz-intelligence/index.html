04 • CORE ENGINEERING MECHANISMS & GOVERNANCE INVARIANTS ZERO-OVERHEAD ENGINE SPECIFICATION

Core Engineering Mechanics & Operational Invariants

MECHANISM 01 • UNIFIED 8-DEPARTMENT DIGITAL LEDGER

Governing All Corporate Functions on a Single Native Backbone

• Zero-Silo Departmental Alignment: Traditional companies suffer from data fragmentation between Accounting, HR, Legal/RA, Procurement, and Sales. Biz Intelligence unifies all 8 core corporate divisions onto a single Google Workspace-native relational ledger.
• Multi-Entity, Multi-Currency Precision: Operating across Korea, Indonesia, Vietnam, Singapore, China, Japan, the USA, and India requires seamless handling of local VAT rules, withholding taxes, and inter-company transfer pricing—all automated via deterministic ledger rules.

MECHANISM 02 • AUTONOMOUS TALENT LEDGER & VENTURE BLUEPRINTING

Vibe Working Attribution & Instant Project Instantiation

• Task-Module HR & Automated 25th Clearing: Instead of managing attendance bureaucracy, Biz Intelligence tracks concrete task-module completion and value contribution—automatically calculating base pay, project incentives, and partner splits for error-free disbursement every 25th of the month.
• One-Spark Venture Instantiation: When leadership initiates a new business concept, the system automatically generates the structural PRD, financial pro-forma, and departmental checklists required to move from idea to execution immediately.

05 • ARCHITECTURAL GOVERNANCE & NON-NEGOTIABLE INVARIANTS

Back-Office Governance, Legal Ring-Fencing & Audit Invariants

Biz Intelligence enforces three institutional governance invariants: (1) Dual-Track Legal & Financial Isolation (strict separation between the IP/holding core and individual operating ventures so project liabilities never contaminate headquarters), (2) Single Source of Truth (zero manual copy-pasting across spreadsheets; every transaction is recorded once and propagated via webhooks), and (3) 100% Audit Traceability (every approval, expense, and payout is permanently timestamped and linked to its originating task ID).
