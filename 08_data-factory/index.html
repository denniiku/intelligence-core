N_{\text{total}} = \sum_{k=1}^{M} \int_{0}^{W} \frac{C_k(t)}{\tau_{\text{req}}(k, t) + \mathbb{E}[\Delta t_{\text{human}}]} \cdot \left( 1 - P_{\text{block}}(k, t) \right) \, dt \ge 2,800,000
